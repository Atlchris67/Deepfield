# Real-system campaigns (isolated integration slice)

Choose **Nearby real star systems** in Configure star map to start a separate Development file-store campaign. The pinned NASA Exoplanet Archive snapshot contains 24 nearby systems and 31 confirmed planets. Catalog version 1, stable system/planet indices, orbital values, playable links, and homeworld assignments are saved with that campaign. Aurora and Ember remain clan names regardless of which real planet hosts them. Existing fictional campaigns and their topology remain on their existing rules.

The map uses real catalog names and projected sky positions. Playable links form a connected game graph; they do not claim feasible astronomical travel. Each clan begins at a named cataloged planet. The star system is the unit of ownership and conquest. The catalog planets contribute fictional materials to their owner's system but are not separately conquerable. A known real star name and position is public; planets in unknown systems and strategic resources are omitted from the clan API view. Enemy colors from recon represent dated owner observations, not guaranteed current ownership.

An expedition can carry up to 100 existing scouts from an owned colony alongside its combat ships. Scouts do not fight or found the destination colony. They land only if the clan holds the destination after combat and a fleet survived there, including ships that became colony habitats. Otherwise the traveling scouts are lost. Landed scouts can launch reconnaissance from that colony on the next turn. Expedition and reconnaissance orders reserve the same source scout inventory; scouts built this turn cannot travel yet.

Every campaign opens with a rotatable sky map as the primary command-deck view. Real-system campaigns use the pinned Milky Way coordinates; fictional campaigns project their saved map systems onto a display sphere without changing their names, links, or saves. Lines show links that can be traversed in one turn. System names beside the markers open the fog-limited dossier; hollow circles have no scouting report, and scouted enemy ownership appears red. Fictional campaigns can still open the separate reference atlas.

## Materials

The six saved resource keys, in order, are Ferrite (structure), Vardite (armor), Ceryth (electronics), Neralite (propellant), Soryl (field systems), and Avenite (rare infrastructure catalyst). These are fiction, not NASA mineral findings. Each catalog planet has one fictional material type derived from private, persisted campaign entropy and stable system/planet IDs. A public star catalog cannot reveal hidden deposits. An owned system gains material from each planet each turn, based on settlement size, `1 + mines`, and its clan's industrial focus. Homeworlds start at settlement size 2 and one mine; new colonies start at size 1. Mine and settlement orders increase these up to four each.

Real campaigns start with `[18, 12, 12, 14, 12, 8]` of those materials per clan or per homeworld, according to the selected material-access mode. Ships cost one Ferrite and Neralite each; scouts cost one Ceryth and Neralite each; fixed defenses cost two Ferrite and one Vardite; collectors cost one Ceryth and Soryl; a mine costs two Ferrite; settlement growth costs two Ferrite and one Avenite. Existing credit costs still apply. Full order lists are checked against both credit and material stock before submission, then consumed once during turn resolution. Collector energy remains the victory measure and is not mined.

## Campaign rules options

**Local stock, freighters and paired gates** is the default for new real-system campaigns. **Shared clan stock** remains a simpler selectable option. In local mode, each homeworld begins with its own stock, an operational gate and two freighters. New outposts start without stock or a gate. A freighter costs 10 credits, two Ferrite, one Ceryth and two Neralite. It carries up to ten material units along one playable link per turn to an already owned system. Cargo arrives after combat and can fund orders next turn. A gate costs 30 credits, four Ferrite, two Ceryth and two Avenite. A gate must be built from stock already accessible at that outpost, normally delivered by freighter. Linked owned systems share available stock only when both gates are operational. A gate completed this turn joins the network next turn. Capture destroys local stock, freighters and gate infrastructure. No resource can be spent twice across orders in one turn.

Each clan has a literal industrial-focus slider from -2 to +2, saved with the campaign. Zero is balanced. Positive focus raises mining output but also raises each ship's credit price; negative focus favors cheaper ships but lowers mining output. The server enforces bounds and applies an output factor of `(4 + focus) / 4`, rounded up per planet with a minimum of one, and adds `focus` to each ship's normal credit price. The choice is independent of astronomy names. Existing campaigns load with neutral focus and the old pooled rules.

This slice does **not** implement Dyson-sphere construction, food management, a full race designer, research, ship design, or broad complexity presets. AI difficulty is separate from these rules. Generated campaigns remain Development file-store only.
